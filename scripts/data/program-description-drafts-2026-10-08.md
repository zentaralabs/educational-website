## 1. university of technology sydney: Master of Artificial Intelligence

42 impressions (3 months) · current 83 words → draft 138 words · source: https://www.uts.edu.au/courses/master-of-artificial-intelligence

The Master of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) is a two-year, full-time coursework degree taught at the City campus in Ultimo (CRICOS 108843D). It covers data analytics and visualisation, machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, reinforcement learning and ethics in AI, with specialised subjects in computer vision and natural language processing.

The course is aimed at graduates in computing science, IT or computer engineering, and includes hands-on work on research and industry projects, so it suits students heading either into AI engineering roles or towards academic research. Applicants who do not meet the entry requirements may be considered for the Graduate Diploma in Artificial Intelligence, which articulates into this master's. Graduates are eligible to apply for professional-level membership of the Australian Computer Society. English requirements are IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with 6.0 in writing.

## 2. university of technology sydney: Master of Data Science and Innovation

40 impressions (3 months) · current 83 words → draft 132 words · source: https://www.uts.edu.au/courses/master-of-data-science-and-innovation

The Master of Data Science and Innovation at the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) is taught by the TD School, which takes a transdisciplinary approach: students from different industries and academic backgrounds learn together. Study takes about 18 months full-time at the City campus, mixing online and in-person sessions. The first year includes Data Science for Innovation, Statistical Thinking for Data Science and Machine Learning Algorithms and Applications, alongside electives.

The curriculum is co-designed with industry partners and reviewed regularly. Students work on real data sets throughout, including iLab projects where they tackle data problems brought in by partner organisations. UTS lists career outcomes such as data scientist, machine learning engineer, data visualiser, big data engineer and data and insights manager. English requirements are IELTS Academic 6.5 overall with 6.0 in writing.

## 3. university of melbourne: Bachelor of Science

40 impressions (3 months) · current 76 words → draft 148 words · source: https://study.unimelb.edu.au/find/courses/undergraduate/bachelor-of-science/

The Bachelor of Science at the University of Melbourne is a three-year degree with more than 40 majors across science, biomedicine, engineering, IT and mathematics. The first year is deliberately broad, so students can sample several fields before choosing a major, and breadth subjects in areas such as business, languages and communication sit alongside the science core. Students complete 24 subjects in total, starting with the discovery subject Today's Science, Tomorrow's World.

Learning combines lectures with laboratory work, fieldwork and research. Students can take the Science and Technology Internship or the Industry Internship subject, each with at least 80 hours of work placement, and every student is matched with an academic adviser to help plan study and graduate options. Students also get access to career workshops, job listings, interview practice and industry events, and graduates move into careers across science, health, engineering and technology or into graduate study.

## 4. university of notre dame australia: Bachelor of Nursing

32 impressions (3 months) · current 74 words → draft 145 words · source: https://www.notredame.edu.au/programs/school-of-nursing/undergraduate/bachelor-of-nursing

The Bachelor of Nursing at the University of Notre Dame Australia is a three-year, full-time degree offered at the Fremantle and Sydney campuses. It is accredited by the Australian Nursing and Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC), and graduates are eligible to apply for registration as a registered nurse with the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia.

Teaching is in small groups, with a strong focus on the nurse-patient relationship. Students build clinical skills in simulation wards, consulting rooms and laboratories before moving into hospital and community placements, and the final semester includes elective specialities. The curriculum also includes Aboriginal health content and professional electives that integrate nursing with philosophy or theology. Notre Dame limits nursing enrolments so every student can be placed. Its course page states that applications are closed for Semester 2 2026 and Semester 1 2027, so check the next open intake before planning.

## 5. university of the sunshine coast: Bachelor of Education (Primary)

31 impressions (3 months) · current 17 words → draft 131 words · source: https://www.unisc.edu.au/study/courses-and-programs/bachelor-degrees-undergraduate-programs/bachelor-of-primary-education

The Bachelor of Education (Primary) at the University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) is a four-year degree that qualifies graduates to teach in primary schools from Prep to Year 6 (CRICOS 073277M). The program is 32 courses (384 units), delivered in blended mode with intakes in Trimester 1 and Trimester 2.

Students complete 80 days of supervised teaching placements in local schools, with rural and remote placements available, and choose a discipline specialisation in either STEM or English and literacy. Before graduating, students must pass the Graduate Teacher Performance Assessment during their final placement. Placements in Queensland schools also require a Blue Card (working with children check). Graduates may be eligible for registration with the Queensland College of Teachers. International students on a student visa must study on campus and full-time.

The Master of Nursing Practice (Preregistration) at the University of New England (UNE) is a two-year, professional-entry degree for people who already hold a bachelor's degree in another field and want to become a registered nurse. The Bachelor of Behavioural Science (Psychology) at the University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) is a three-year, 24-course degree (CRICOS 110837F) offered at the Sunshine Coast and Moreton Bay campuses. The Australian Psychology Accreditation Council (APAC) has accredited it as a three-year undergraduate sequence in psychology.

Students study how people develop across the lifespan, motivation, personality and behaviour change, and cover cognitive, social, perceptual, physiological, intercultural, Indigenous and abnormal psychology. Electives allow specialisation in other health science areas, and students may work as research assistants. Becoming a registered psychologist in Australia requires an accredited honours year plus further accredited postgraduate study, so this degree is the first step on that path rather than a full qualification. UniSC lists graduate roles in community work, disability support, health and education services, human resources, research, and training and development.

The Master of International Economics and Finance at the University of Queensland (UQ) is taught at the St Lucia campus in Brisbane by Queensland's largest School of Economics. It combines two study areas, international economics and finance, so graduates can work across public policy and financial markets. The standard program is two years full-time (32 units) for applicants with a bachelor's degree in any discipline. ## 11. university of new england: Bachelor of Education (Early Childhood and Primary)

17 impressions (3 months) · current 84 words → draft 148 words · source: https://www.une.edu.au/study/courses/bachelor-of-education-early-childhood-and-primary

The Bachelor of Education (Early Childhood and Primary) at the University of New England (UNE) is a four-year initial teacher education degree that qualifies graduates to teach children from birth to 12 years, in either early childhood services or primary schools. Every unit is core, giving equal weight to early childhood and primary teacher education.

Students complete 135 days of professional experience: 75 days in primary schools and 60 days in early childhood centres. Students already working in an approved early childhood service can usually complete those placements in-service. The degree is accredited by the NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA) and the Australian Children's Education and Care Quality Authority (ACECQA). Most students study online across UNE's three study periods. UNE's course page states that this degree is currently not available to international students, so it is an option only for Australian and New Zealand citizens and permanent residents.

The Bachelor of Sport and Exercise Science at the University of the Sunshine Coast (UniSC) is a three-year degree accredited at the level of Exercise Science by Exercise and Sports Science Australia (ESSA), and graduates may identify themselves as exercise scientists. ## 16. australian national university: Master of Economic Policy

The Master of Economic Policy at the Australian National University (ANU) in Canberra is a two-year, full-time program (96 units) run by the Research School of Economics in the ANU College of Business and Economics. It trains students to analyse the economic effects of policy changes and communicate them as professional economists, combining theoretical, empirical and data analysis skills with sustained exposure to real policy issues.

Applicants with a cognate bachelor's degree or graduate certificate may receive up to one semester of credit, and those with a cognate graduate diploma or honours degree up to one year. ## 17. federation university australia: Bachelor of Social Work

12 impressions (3 months) · current 75 words → draft 138 words · source: https://www.federation.edu.au/courses/dsw5-bachelor-of-social-work/

The Bachelor of Social Work at Federation University Australia is a four-year degree offered on campus at Berwick, Gippsland and Mt Helen (Ballarat), with intakes in March and July. It has provisional accreditation with the Australian Association of Social Workers (AASW). The course was co-designed with local industry partners to address a shortage of social workers in the region.

Students complete 140 days of placement in social work settings and learn to work with people facing substance use, mental health problems, palliative care, child protection and youth services. Where possible, Federation works with students' existing employers so they can keep working while they study. Graduates work in government, non-government and community organisations in counselling, advocacy, community development, policy and research, and experienced social workers in clinical roles can later apply to become an Accredited Mental Health Social Worker.

## 23. james cook university: Juris Doctor

The Juris Doctor at James Cook University (JCU) is a graduate-entry law degree for students who already hold a bachelor's degree in another field. The Bachelor of Nursing at Avondale University is a three-year degree (six semesters full-time) accredited by the Australian Nursing and Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC). Graduates are eligible to apply for registration as a registered nurse.
