# ACU Admission to Coursework Programs Policy - Schedule 4 (English Language Proficiency)
Source: https://policy.acu.edu.au/document/view.php?id=312 (current version, fetched 2026-09-24)

## Band table (Evidence -> IELTS Academic / PTE Academic / TOEFL iBT / C1 Advanced / ACU test)
- Band A: IELTS overall 6.0, no band below 5.5 | PTE 50 (min 42 all four) | TOEFL iBT 60 (min 18 writing/8 reading/16 speaking/7 listening) | C1 Advanced 169 (min 169 writing/speaking, 162 listening/reading) | ACU EAP C (60-62%)
- Band B: IELTS overall 6.0 (6.0 writing/speaking, 5.5 listening/reading) | PTE 50 (min 50 writing/speaking, 42 reading/listening) | TOEFL iBT 60 (min 21 writing, 18 speaking, 8 reading, 7 listening) | C1 Advanced 176 (min 169 all) | ACU EAP C (63-64%)
- Band C: IELTS overall 6.5, no band below 6.0 | PTE 58 (min 50 all four) | TOEFL iBT 79 (min 21 writing, 18 speaking, 13 reading, 12 listening) | C1 Advanced 185 (min 176 all) | ACU EAP B (65-74%)
- Band D: IELTS overall 7.0, no band below 6.0 | PTE 65 (min 50 all four) | TOEFL iBT 94 (min 21 writing, 18 speaking, 13 reading, 12 listening) | C1 Advanced 185 (min 169 all) | ACU EAP A (75-79%)
- Band E: IELTS overall 7.0, no band below 6.5 | PTE 65 (min 58 all four) | TOEFL iBT 94 (min 24 writing, 20 speaking/listening, 19 reading) | C1 Advanced 185 (min 176 all) | ACU EAP A (75-79%)
- Band F1: IELTS overall 7.0 (7.0 listening/reading/speaking, 6.5 writing) [excl. Nursing/Midwifery] | PTE 66 (min 66 LRS, min 56 writing) | TOEFL iBT 94 (min 24 writing/reading/listening, 23 speaking) | C1 Advanced 185 (min 185 LRS, 176 writing) | ACU EAP A (75-79%)
- Band F2 (tests taken on/after 23 Apr 2026): IELTS overall 7.0 (7.0 LRS, 6.5 writing) | PTE 63 (min 58 listening/59 reading/60 writing/76 speaking) | TOEFL iBT 91 (min 22/22/23/24) | C1 Advanced 178 | ACU EAP A (75-79%)
- Band G: IELTS overall 7.0, individual 7.0 all tests [excl. Postgrad Psychology, which doesn't accept C1 Advanced] | PTE 65 (min 65 all four) | TOEFL iBT 94 (min 27 writing/23 speaking/24 reading&listening) | C1 Advanced 185 (min 185 all) | ACU EAP A (75-79%)
- Band H: IELTS min 7.0 reading/writing, min 7.5 listening/speaking | PTE min 65 reading/writing, min 73 listening/speaking | TOEFL iBT min 24 reading/27 writing/27 listening/24 speaking | C1 Advanced min 185 reading/writing, min 191 listening/speaking | ACU EAP A+ (80-84)
- Band I: IELTS no score below 7 reading/writing, no score below 8 listening/speaking | PTE min 65 reading/writing, min 79 listening/speaking | TOEFL iBT no score below 24 reading/27 writing/28 listening/26 speaking | C1 Advanced min 185 reading/writing, 200 listening/speaking | ACU EAP A++ (85+)

## Course family -> Band mapping (as published)
- Certificates, Undergraduate Certificates, Diplomas (excl. Philosophy/Theology & Diploma in Educational Studies Tertiary Preparation), Associate Degrees -> Band A
- Foundation Studies -> "Refer to specific requirements"
- Diplomas in Theology and Philosophy Courses -> "Refer to specific entry requirements"
- Tertiary Preparation Program (Health Sciences) -> "Refer to specific entry requirements"
- Bachelor Degrees (unless specified below), Incoming Study Abroad (Undergrad) -> Band B
- Incoming Study Abroad (Undergrad) with concurrent language study -> Band A
- Incoming Study Abroad (Postgrad), Bachelor of Arts (Western Civilisation), Postgraduate Digital and Human Futures Courses -> Band C
- Undergraduate Initial Teacher Education Courses except Bachelor of Early Childhood Education (Birth to Five Years); Diploma in Educational Studies (Tertiary Preparation) -> Band H
- Graduate Entry Initial Teacher Education courses incl. vertical degrees; Bachelor of Arts (Western Civilisation) (Honours) -> Band I
- Bachelor of Early Childhood Education (Birth to Five Years); Other Health Sciences Courses (not set out below); All Postgraduate Business; All Postgraduate Theology and Philosophy; All Postgraduate Arts -> Band C
- Postgraduate Continuing Education Courses -> Band D
- All Law courses -> Band E
- Master of Clinical Exercise Physiology; Master of Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy; Master of Sports and Exercise Physiotherapy/Master of High Performance Sport -> Band E
- Bachelor of Speech Pathology -> "Refer to specific entry requirements" (ACU English Language test / ACU EAP NOT accepted)
- Bachelor of Nursing (+ combined Nursing degrees), Bachelor of Midwifery -> Band F1 (tests taken on/before 22 Apr 2026) / Band F2 (on/after 23 Apr 2026)
- Bachelor of Paramedicine; Bachelor of Psychological Science (+ double degrees); Bachelor of Psychology (Honours) -> Band F1
- Bachelor of Occupational Therapy; Bachelor of Physiotherapy; Bachelor of Social Work; Master of Dietetic Practice; Master of Social Work (Qualifying); Postgraduate Psychology courses -> Band G

Notes: OET accepted for undergrad Nursing (meets NMBA standard). Ahpra-registered professions must independently meet Ahpra's own English standard. This table plus the Band definitions is the single, real, authoritative source for `english_requirements` fields for ALL ACU rows - do not invent a per-course IELTS number outside this table.
